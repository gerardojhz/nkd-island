# El Secreto de NKD Island

**[▶ Play it in your browser](https://gerardojhz.github.io/nkd-island/)** (Spanish)

A point-and-click pirate adventure built in a single HTML file, in the spirit of
the classic LucasArts games. You play Captain *Invierno Funk*, who has to pass three
pirate trials to recruit a crew and set sail: a battle of wits against
*Don Corporativo* (insult-sword-fighting with corporate jargon), a hidden treasure
behind a dial puzzle, and getting past the dock guard.

Starring Chato & Jerry, the founders of Grupo NKD.

## How I made it

- **Game logic, dialogue and UI:** built with Claude. One self-contained
  `index.html`, no frameworks, no build step: scenes, hotspots, inventory with item
  combination, a dialogue system, the wit duel and the dial puzzle.
- **Pixel art and music:** generated with Higgsfield.

## Play locally

Download `index.html` and open it in Chrome, Safari or Edge.
Click to explore and talk, click an item in the inventory and then a place to use it,
and click two items to combine them. Press `H` to show the hotspots.
